# 🏦 Finance-Bandhan Bank — End-to-End Business Intelligence & Analytics Project
About Bandhan Bank

Bandhan Bank was established in 2001 as a not-for-profit organization with the objective of promoting financial inclusion and women’s empowerment.

It initially focused on providing microfinance services to underserved communities, particularly through group-based lending.

The organization offers various types of loans to groups, including:

Monthly Loans
Weekly Loans
Individual Lending through Group Formation

Its primary focus is to provide accessible financial services to low-income households and support their economic empowerment and financial inclusion.
