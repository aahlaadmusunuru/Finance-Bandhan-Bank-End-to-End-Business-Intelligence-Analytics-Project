# 🏦 Finance-Bandhan Bank — End-to-End Business Intelligence & Analytics Project
## **About Bandhan Bank Project**

Bandhan Bank was established in **2001** with the objective of promoting **financial inclusion and women’s empowerment**. It initially focused on providing **microfinance services to underserved communities through group-based lending**. The bank offers different types of loans, including **weekly and monthly loans**, where members of a group are responsible for regular repayments. The project focuses on understanding this lending process and designing a **database, data model, and reporting solution** to manage customers, groups, loans, repayments, and related information.
